# North American (US/Canada) Vertical Micro-Drama Market, 2024–2026

> Method note: As of 2026-10-05, every source below came from web-search result snippets. Direct page fetches to Sensor Tower, Variety, TheWrap, Reuters mirrors, Wikipedia and Marketing Dive were blocked by the network proxy. Figures are given as the snippets reported them, with outlet and date where known. Low-tier aggregator/blog sources are flagged as such.

## 1. Dominant themes and why (link to romance webnovel apps)

### Takeaway
US micro-drama content is mostly female-targeted romance and wish-fulfilment. The main tropes are the billionaire/hidden-identity CEO, werewolf Alpha/"rejected mate becomes Luna", vampire, mafia romance, revenge (often on a cheating spouse), rebirth, and pregnancy or contract marriage. These tropes come straight from the English-language romance webnovel ecosystem (GoodNovel, Dreame, WebNovel, ReelShort Novel), which serves as the IP pipeline. Romance still dominates (76% of female and 54% of male viewing on leading platforms, per Holywater/Owl & Co, Jul 2026). Genres are now widening toward thriller, fantasy, sci-fi and horror to reach men.

### Cited Findings
- ReelShort describes its own catalogue as "werewolf and billionaire romances to mafia tales and campus love" — [ReelShort.com](https://www.reelshort.com/)
- ReelShort's themes "lean heavily into billionaire romances, supernatural storylines (werewolves and vampires), and revenge arcs". Typical plots are "a secret billionaire CEO, a reborn heroine taking revenge, or a rejected mate who turns out to be Luna". Most Chinese-origin microdramas adapt web novels, so the web-novel catalogue works as the content pipeline for ReelShort and DramaBox — [TeaNovel blog, 2026 (low-tier)](https://read.teanovel.com/blog/chinese-web-novel-short-drama-adaptations); [StartupHub.ai, 2026 (low-tier)](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/ai-tools/2026/microdramas-explained-the-8-billion-vertical-drama-boom-and-the-new-adult-ai-frontier)
- GoodNovel (an English romance webnovel app from the same corporate family as GoodShort, Xinyue) runs dedicated "Werewolf + CEO + Pregnant" tag pages. WebNovel hosts titles such as "The Werewolf CEO That Got Me Pregnant". The tropes match micro-drama titles like "Pregnant and Rejected: The Alpha's Cruel Choice" — [GoodNovel](https://www.goodnovel.com/stories/werewolf-16-ceo_79-pregnant_51-werewolf_16-novels); [WebNovel](https://m.webnovel.com/subject/677657330602541056)
- ReelShort's parent Crazy Maple Studio also runs a "ReelShort Novel" app, which shows a novel-to-drama pipeline under one roof — [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6636532586)
- Fox Entertainment Studios' first two titles for My Drama were "Billionaire Blackmail" and "Bound by Obsession", shot in Atlanta (Oct 2025). This shows US studios adopting the same tropes — [Deadline, Oct 2025](https://deadline.com/2025/10/fox-invests-vertical-video-holywater-1236574693/); [THR, Oct 2025](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/fox-entertainment-invests-in-holywater-ai-microdramas-1236396802/)
- Romance accounts for 76% of female viewing and 54% of male viewing on leading platforms. Genres are expanding into sci-fi and fantasy, and horror/thriller is "underserved" but growing. Viewing peaks on Sunday nights, 8–10 pm — [Variety, Jul 2026](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/microdramas-men-romance-sunday-holywater-my-drama-my-muse-1236819953/); [GlobeNewswire, 23 Jul 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/23/3332522/0/en/HOLYWATER-TECH-and-Owl-Co-Publish-First-Industry-Report-on-the-Vertical-Series-Audience.html)
- NetShort is positioned on thriller, in contrast with DramaBox's romance focus — [Streaming Lens (low-tier)](https://lens.streaming-radar.com/netshort-vs-dramabox)
- Paywalls are placed at peak narrative moments to drive impulse purchases "driven by short-term emotional highs" — [MIDiA Research](https://www.midiaresearch.com/blog/can-reelshort-succeed-with-short-form-video-entertainment-where-quibi-failed) / [IndieWire](https://www.indiewire.com/news/business/reelshort-not-quibi-its-candy-crush-analysis-1234949616/) (summarised from search snippet)

### Inferences
- These tropes exist because a large, data-tested audience of US women already paid per chapter for werewolf/CEO/revenge stories on webnovel apps (Dreame, GoodNovel, WebNovel) from roughly 2018 onward. Short drama apps carried over both the IP and the pay-per-unlock model. Tropes with fast cliffhangers and "face-slap" reversals suit episodes of about 1–2 minutes behind a paywall.
- The rise in male viewers (see Section 2) will probably push catalogues toward thriller, action and "system"/fantasy genres in 2026–27.

### Gaps
- I found no quantitative US genre-share breakdown (e.g., % of top-grossing titles by trope) from Sensor Tower or Appfigures.
- I could not retrieve a direct Dreame-specific source tying Dreame IP to particular US dramas.

## 2. Audience: who watches, spending behavior, willingness to pay

### Takeaway
The core US audience is women aged about 25–55, and the heaviest segment is 35–54. Reuters (Aug 2026) and Jen Cooper's survey of more than 1,600 viewers agree on this. The audience is getting younger and more male: on Holywater's apps, men rose from 1.1% to about 30% of MAU in two years. US MAU more than doubled, from 26M (2024) to 66M (2025). Engagement is unusually high: ReelShort averages 35.7 minutes per day versus 24.8 for Netflix mobile. Spending is high per head: weekly subscriptions run about $8–20, and finishing one series costs about $18–40.

### Cited Findings
- "Women aged 25 to 55 make up the core user base." US microdrama MAU was 66M last year, more than double the 26M in 2024 — [Reuters via Investing.com, Aug 2026](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/microdramas-boom-in-a-shrinking-hollywood-as-studios-chase-a-tiktok-audience-4864722); [BusinessWorld (Reuters), 21 Aug 2026](https://bworldonline.com/arts-and-leisure/2026/08/21/771426/microdramas-boom-in-a-shrinking-hollywood-as-studios-chase-a-tiktok-audience/)
- In Jen Cooper's survey of more than 1,600 micro-drama viewers, more than half were aged 35–54 and the vast majority were female — [Digital Content Next, 5 Mar 2026](https://digitalcontentnext.org/blog/2026/03/05/how-microdramas-hook-viewers-and-drive-revenue/) (via search snippet)
- On YouTube microdrama channels, women 35–44 accounted for 20.8% of streams and women 45–54 for 15.7%. That is more than double their 7.7% share of overall YouTube viewing — [Señal News, Series Mania 2026](https://senalnews.com/en/data/series-mania-2026-women-35-boost-youtube-microdrama-growth)
- Holywater/Owl & Co "State of Microdrama 2026" (23 Jul 2026), based on My Drama/My Muse product data plus a May 2026 survey of 2,737 users:
  - Male share of MAU grew from 1.1% (Q2/late 2024) to 30.3%, which the report calls a 28-fold rise in seven quarters.
  - Viewers aged 22–27 nearly tripled their viewing share over five quarters and now make up about 25% of My Drama's audience.
  - The report says: "In 2024, the microdrama audience was caricatured as … women, 35-54, watching at night … still female-dominant. But … increasingly, male, younger, and global."
  - Paid conversion is 23.9% on My Drama (live action) and 23.6% on My Muse (AI-generated).
  - Sources: [Deadline, Jul 2026](https://deadline.com/2026/07/microdrama-report-holywater-audience-younger-male-1237001059/); [Variety, Jul 2026](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/microdramas-men-romance-sunday-holywater-my-drama-my-muse-1236819953/); [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/23/3332522/0/en/HOLYWATER-TECH-and-Owl-Co-Publish-First-Industry-Report-on-the-Vertical-Series-Audience.html)
- Daily minutes per user in the US (Q4 2025 Sensor Tower data, analysed by Omdia): ReelShort 35.7, Amazon Prime Video 26.9, Netflix 24.8, Disney+ 23.0. On reach, Netflix has about 12M US monthly active mobile users versus 1.1M for ReelShort. Omdia: "Microdramas are winning the battle for attention, rather than scale" — [Omdia press release, 23 Feb 2026](https://omdia.tech.informa.com/pr/2026/feb/microdramas-overtake-streamers-on-mobile-engagement-says-omdia); [Deadline, Feb 2026](https://deadline.com/2026/02/microdrama-more-time-cellphones-streaming-services-omdia-1236732921/); [Broadband TV News, 23 Feb 2026](https://www.broadbandtvnews.com/2026/02/23/microdramas-overtake-streamers-on-us-mobile-engagement-says-omdia/)
- Pricing and spend:
  - The first few episodes are free, then weekly subscriptions cost about $8–20.
  - Many viewers spend $20–40 to finish one series, and one influencer spends up to $200 a month.
  - Sources: [Reuters via Tribune, Aug 2026](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2624560/microdramas-boom-in-a-shrinking-hollywood-as-studios-chase-a-tiktok-audience) (snippet; attribution of the spend figures within the article is unclear)
- Coins: an episode costs about $0.18 in virtual currency, so binging a 100-episode series costs about $18 — [MIDiA Research](https://www.midiaresearch.com/blog/can-reelshort-succeed-with-short-form-video-entertainment-where-quibi-failed) (snippet)

### Inferences
- High willingness to pay comes from a small, intensely engaged base. ReelShort's US monthly reach is about 1/10 of Netflix mobile's, but its time per user is higher. Revenue therefore depends on heavy spenders ("whales") in the 35–54 female segment.
- A 23–24% paid conversion rate (Holywater) is very high for a consumer media app, but it is self-reported by Holywater.

### Gaps
- I found no Canada-specific audience or revenue data. Canada is usually folded into "North America" or left out.
- I found no ARPPU or LTV by demographic from a neutral third party.
- Reuters' 66M MAU figure cites an unnamed source in the snippet. It is far above Omdia's 1.1M figure for ReelShort alone, but the two measure different things (category-wide vs. one app on mobile).

## 3. Apps and their performance (downloads, revenue, rankings)

### Takeaway
Chinese-backed apps dominate. ReelShort (Crazy Maple Studio, partly owned by COL Group/中文在线) and DramaBox (Dianzhong Tech/点众) are a clear #1 and #2, each making about $130–140M per quarter in global IAP in 2025–Q1 2026. They are followed by DramaWave (Skywork/昆仑万维), NetShort, GoodShort (Xinyue/新阅) and ShortMax (Jiuzhou/九州文化). Media Partners Asia's share estimates for ex-China (Aug 2026) are ReelShort 29%, DramaBox 21%, DramaWave 13%, NetShort 10%, GoodShort 6%.

US-native and Hollywood entrants are newer:
- Holywater/My Drama: $22M raise in Jan 2026, plus a Fox equity stake.
- TikTok PineDrama: launched Jan 2026.
- Disney+: Verts feed and the "Locker Diaries" micro-drama.
- Amazon and Netflix: vertical feeds.

## 4. US market revenue, share of global overseas revenue, growth trajectory

### Takeaway
The US is the largest non-China market, at about 45–50% of overseas short-drama IAP. US micro-drama revenue went from about $350M in Q1 2025 to about $1.3B for 2025. Omdia expects $1.5B in 2026 (50% of ex-China), and MPA projects about $3.7B by 2031. Global (ex-China) app IAP was about $2.8–3.0B in 2025, up about 115%. Growth decelerated sharply in Q1 2026, to +20% YoY globally.

### Cited Findings
- Q1 2025: US short-drama IAP was nearly $350M, +20% QoQ and 49% of global revenue. Global Q1 2025 IAP was about $700M (roughly 3x YoY), with 370M+ downloads (6.2x YoY) and about 950M cumulative downloads by March 2025 — [Sensor Tower 2025](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-short-drama-apps-2025); [Webull/Sensor Tower](https://www.webull.ca/news-detail/12816921610159104)
- Full-year 2025 global short-drama app IAP was $2.98B (+115% YoY, the third-fastest-growing category Sensor Tower measured), with 2.3B downloads — [Sensor Tower 2025 report (snippet)](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-short-drama-apps-2025). This conflicts with NBD's citation of Sensor Tower at $2.8B (+116%) — [NBD on X, Jan 2026](https://x.com/NBDPress/status/2011684784469402090)
- Q1 2026 global IAP was about $750M, only +20% YoY, which is a sharp slowdown from roughly 3x a year earlier — [Sensor Tower 2026 report](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-short-drama-apps-2026-report)

### Inferences
- The rough US trajectory is: 2023 under $0.3B (ReelShort alone made $97M), 2024 about $0.6–0.8B, 2025 about $1.3B, 2026E $1.5B, 2027E about $2B, 2031E about $3.7B. Growth is moving from triple digits (2024–25) to about 15–35% a year. This combines different methodologies, so treat it as directional only.
- The US share of overseas revenue has stayed stable at about 45–50% since 2024 even as Asia, LatAm and Europe grow. The US is the profit pool, while other markets drive download growth.

## 5. Production in LA: costs, SAG-AFTRA, industry reception

### Takeaway
LA and Atlanta have become hubs for English-language micro-drama shoots. A 60–100 episode series costs about $100K–300K and is shot in about 7–14 days. SAG-AFTRA formalised the work with a Verticals Agreement on 13 Oct 2025, covering budgets under $300K. Hollywood treats the format as one of its few growth areas while being skeptical of its quality ("dramaslop", "boxed wine").

### Cited Findings
- An LA vertical drama costs $150K–200K for a 60–100 episode series shot in 8–10 days. More broadly, series cost $100K–300K with 7–14 day shoots — [Filmustage, 2026 (low-tier)](https://filmustage.com/blog/how-to-produce-a-vertical-drama-on-a-budget/) (via search summary)

- The SAG-AFTRA deal, with its low minimums and an experimental sunset, signals that the union chose to legitimise and capture work that was largely non-union, rather than fight it.
