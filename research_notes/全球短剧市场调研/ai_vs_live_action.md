## 1. Volume and share of AI dramas on platforms; hit titles and view counts

### Takeaway
By 2026, AI dramas dominate **supply** in China. Reports put them at more than 90–95% of new microdrama releases (Q1–Q3 2026). They also climbed charts quickly: 7% of the Douyin/漫剧 top-100 in Jan 2025, 38% in Jan 2026, and 58% of the Douyin 漫剧 top-100 in Feb 2026. In April 2026 an AI title topped Hongguo's merged overall chart for the first time. Their share of **plays** is much smaller than their share of supply, and figures for it conflict.

## 2. Comparative performance: average plays, completion, ARPU/payment, ratings, and audience attitudes

### Takeaway
I found no clean apples-to-apples dataset comparing AI and live-action dramas on average plays, completion or ARPU. Surveys and commentary show strong discomfort with "AI脸" (the "AI face" look) and the uncanny valley in China, yet top AI titles still win top-chart traffic. Overseas, one operator's data shows AI series convert to paid at the same rate as live-action.

### Cited Findings
- Survey (2026): only 6.2% of viewers accept all AI techniques. About 82% feel clear discomfort at AI faces, and about 45% of those swipe away. About 15% don't mind as long as the plot is good. More than 74% explicitly reject fully-AI series ("眼神空洞" hollow eyes, "表演机械" mechanical acting) — [新浪 self-media](https://k.sina.com.cn/article_7879922982_1d5ae15260680b86s4.html?from=tech); [搜狐](https://www.sohu.com/a/1050778736_351788). **The survey's sample and publisher are not given; low reliability.**
- 腾讯新闻 "直面AI浪潮⑦" series: "超半数观众排斥" ("more than half of viewers reject") AI film/TV — [腾讯新闻 2026-07-14](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260714A0B1YH00)
- The "AI脸+恐怖谷" (AI face + uncanny valley) topic trended on hot-search lists. Same-face AI faces make "活人感" (a sense of a real person) a scarce resource — [澎湃 2026](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_33589100); [新浪](https://k.sina.com.cn/article_7879922982_1d5ae15260680bbct8.html?from=tech) ("90%的观众看到精致AI脸会感到不适", "90% of viewers feel uncomfortable at polished AI faces", unsourced)
- Completion rate: one source claims AI-made 甜宠/言情 (sweet romance) dramas have the highest completion rate among AI genres at 72%. Single, unattributed source — [新浪 self-media](https://k.sina.com.cn/article_7879848900_1d5acf3c4068037o04.html?from=tech)
- Economics of plays: a "conservative" 漫剧 needs about 4 million effective plays to break even, yet about 80% of works have fewer than 1 million cumulative plays. The revenue ceiling is ¥1–2M, and a phenomenon hit can reach about ¥10M — [21经济网 2026-06-08](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260608/herald/5ca9eebd7814553773d6a8b87dc33142.html); [tmtpost](https://www.tmtpost.com/7905101.html)
- Holywater (My Drama / My Muse), State of Microdrama 2026, July 2026, combining product data with a survey of 2,737 users: on the AI-generated My Muse, 89% of surveyed users want more AI stories. Paid conversion was 23.6% on My Muse (AI) vs 23.9% on My Drama (live-action) — [GlobeNewswire 2026-07-23](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/23/3332522/0/en/HOLYWATER-TECH-and-Owl-Co-Publish-First-Industry-Report-on-the-Vertical-Series-Audience.html); [Holywater report](https://www.holywater.tech/report). (Operator-published and self-selected users.)
- Some media claim that in some genres viewers "can hardly tell" AI from live-action, thanks to Seedance 2.0, Kling 3.0 and Vidu — [SCMP/Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/500-dramas-day-china-ai-112321462.html)

### Inferences
- In China, reception splits in two. A loud majority says it dislikes AI faces, but platform charts show leading AI仿真人 titles drawing comparable traffic. Stated attitudes and actual viewing behavior may diverge, especially among Hongguo 漫剧 users, who are young and male.
- The Holywater result (AI ≈ live-action on conversion) is the only quantitative AI-vs-human monetization comparison I found. It is operator-reported.

### Gaps
- No platform-published comparison of average completion rate, ARPU, pay rate or 豆瓣/app ratings for AI vs live-action.
- No large, methodologically sound third-party audience survey (for example from CNSA (中国网络视听节目服务协会) or 艾瑞 (iResearch)) was found in snippets.

## 3. Cost and production speed

### Takeaway
AI cuts production cost by roughly 80–90% and production time by about 2/3 or more. Typical figures: live-action paid dramas cost ¥100–500k (premium about ¥1.5M), while AI仿真人 costs under ¥200k and entry-level AI ¥300–5,000. AI costs about ¥1,000 per minute, against about ¥20,000 per minute for live-action 仙侠 (xianxia). But marketing and distribution, plus IP, now take a larger share of the budget, and most AI dramas lose money.

### Cited Findings
- Entry-level AI drama: ¥300–5,000 per series. Mid-tier: ¥100–200k. Top-tier: ¥200k–1M. Live-action: ¥5k to more than ¥5M, with mainstream paid titles at ¥100–500k — [搜狐 2026](https://m.sohu.com/a/1038318893_120714299); [知乎, 2026 quotes](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/2051008139324036478)
- By Dec 2025, AI cost about ¥1,000 per minute. Live-action 仙侠 costs close to ¥20,000 per minute (sets, wire work, costume). "一天能上20部" ("20 titles can go live in a day") — [第一财经 Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103084978.html)
- A premium live-action paid drama averages about ¥1.5M. A vertical feed-style AI仿真人 drama can stay under ¥200k, roughly 90% less. Actor fees plus location rental are more than 60% of live-action cost — [360娱乐 / aggregated](https://m.yule.360.com/content/5129924); [新浪财经](https://cj.sina.com.cn/articles/view/7879776328/1d5abd84806801dkd6?froms=ggmp)
- AI costs about one-tenth of a live-action shoot. Firms now spend about 30% of budgets on AI tools, cutting production time from 3 months to 1 and cost to about one-fifth — [SCMP/Yahoo 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/500-dramas-day-china-ai-112321462.html); [JoJo Ventures "90% cost drop"](https://jojo.ventures/chinese-ai-short-dramas/)
- 《山海奇镜》 took 2 months vs 6 months or more estimated for live-action — [woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/share/6127564.html)
- Overseas: "成本从百万元降到万元" ("cost falls from millions of yuan to tens of thousands") — [腾讯新闻 2026-08-27](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260827A0D5NS00). DramaBox reportedly cut per-episode cost from $8K to $2K — [Filmustage](https://filmustage.com/blog/short-drama-apps-compared-reelshort-vs-dramabox-in-2026/)
- Profitability: about 90% of AI漫剧 companies lose money. Top individual creators earn about ¥100k per month. One founder spent about ¥200k on 11 AI漫剧 and earned a few hundred yuan in a month — [tmtpost](https://www.tmtpost.com/7905101.html); [投资界 2026-06](https://news.pedaily.cn/202606/565221.shtml); [文学城](https://www.wenxuecity.com/news/2026/06/15/126679295.html?read=1&subid=news). Yicai: "AI短剧调查：无处安放的产能" ("AI drama investigation: capacity with nowhere to go") — [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103208757.html)
- 36氪 argues that AI漫剧's real edge is ROI plus short cycles: faster capital turnover, serialized "seasons", and IP accumulation — [36氪](https://36kr.com/p/3838601706440965)

### Inferences
- The cost gap is real. But because volume exploded and the hit rate is about 0.12–1%, AI's lower unit cost does not translate into better economics for the typical producer. Returns are winner-take-most.

### Gaps
- No audited per-title P&L comparison. Most cost figures are vendor or self-media quotes.

## 4. Platform policies, regulation and incentives

### Takeaway
Regulation tightened in stages. Mandatory AI labeling began on 2025-09-01. In Nov 2025 NRTA (国家广电总局) brought AIGC and animated microdramas into tiered review. From 2026-04-01 it required filing before release, and from 2026-07-01 a tiered classification standard took effect. By late Sep 2026, NRTA was explicitly calling for more support for live-action. Platforms first subsidized AI heavily, then cut revenue-share coefficients from April 2026.

### Cited Findings
- From 2025-09-01, all AI-generated or AI-assisted audio and video must carry a visible on-screen label plus implicit metadata provenance — [澎湃](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_33643733); [知乎](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/2057504019162063304)
- From Feb 2025, NRTA bars distributing microdramas without a license or filing number shown — [tvoao](https://www.tvoao.com/a/220310.aspx); [RFA 2025-02-06](https://www.rfa.org/mandarin/wenhua/2025/02/06/ccp-control-internet-short-video/)
- Nov 2025: NRTA's microdrama special rectification campaign brought AIGC and 漫画-type (comic-style) animated microdramas into tiered review for the first time. From 2026-04-01, AI microdramas must be filed before release, and unfiled existing animated microdramas are taken down — [腾讯新闻 2026-03-20](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260320A08O2800); [tvoao](http://m.tvoao.com/news/223713.html)
- On 2026-06-25, NRTA's online audiovisual department issued 《管理提示（AI微短剧分类分层标准）》, effective 2026-07-01. Review tiers: investment ≥¥3M or sensitive themes go to NRTA; ¥1–3M to provincial bureaus; <¥1M to platform self-review plus provincial filing — [知乎](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/2057504019162063304); [澎湃](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_33643733)
- CNSA (中国网络视听节目服务协会): "政策护航 AI微短剧迈入发展新阶段" ("policy support moves AI microdramas into a new stage") — [cnsa.cn 2026-08-13](http://www.cnsa.cn/art/2026/8/13/art_1955_49173.html)
- NRTA called for greater support for live-action dramas and quality, citing copyright and creator-pressure concerns — [SCMP ~2026-09](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/politics/article/3369183/slow-beautiful-china-launches-war-against-ai-drama-goes-beyond-algorithms)
- Hongguo/Douyin incentives: Feb 2026 漫剧 policy offered AI仿真人 scripts a guarantee of up to ¥80k, a 20% permanent revenue share, and a coefficient of 60 — [塔猴](https://www.tahou.com/article/200990911852072965); [21经济网 2026-03-10](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260310/herald/67ed9b3cf6b9812a943ea1324fa463ed.html). On 2026-04-30, the Douyin 短剧版权中心 (short-drama copyright center) cut the 仿真人 coefficient from 60 to 40 (−33%) and 3D animation from 50 to 40 — [21经济网 2026-06-08](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260608/herald/5ca9eebd7814553773d6a8b87dc33142.html). Top 仿真人 projects can get up to ¥2M of investment. July 2026: the coefficient for 解说漫剧 (narrated comic dramas) rose from 1 to 10, and 60-minute AI dramas got cash guarantees — [腾讯新闻 2026-05-11](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260511A08RTT00); [geekpark](https://www.geekpark.net/news/368261)
- Counter-signal: Hongguo's 2026 content budget is up more than 40%, with live-action a key focus. The 果燃计划 (Guoran Plan) offers up to ¥2M per project. Headline: "红果加码真人短剧，'AI焦虑'不成立" ("Hongguo doubles down on live-action; the 'AI anxiety' doesn't hold") — [证券时报 STCN](https://www.stcn.com/article/detail/3704091.html); [DoNews](https://www.donews.com/article/detail/8059/101853.html)
- Hongguo revenue-share transparency project starting Q2 2026 — [塔猴](https://www.tahou.com/article/200990911852072965)

### Inferences
- The policy direction since mid-2026 is to contain low-quality AI volume (filing, labeling, coefficient cuts) while supporting premium live-action. This could slow AI supply growth in H2 2026.

### Gaps
- I did not see NRTA's original notice texts; dates come from secondary reports. I found no overseas regulatory equivalent (US/EU labeling rules specific to AI dramas).

## 5. Where AI works vs where live-action dominates, and trends

### Takeaway
AI excels at fantasy, 修仙/玄幻 (cultivation/fantasy), myth, sci-fi, 逆袭 (comeback), 战神 (war god) and 脑洞 (high-concept), where VFX-heavy settings are expensive to shoot. Its audience is male-skewed and young. Live-action keeps an edge in emotional realism and romance and in performance-driven "活人感". There is conflicting evidence that AI 甜宠 is gaining. Trends point to AI仿真人 replacing 2D, chart merging, consolidation, regulation and an overseas expansion.

### Cited Findings
- In DataEye's recent Hongguo 漫剧 hot list, 5 of the top 10 were 修真仙侠 (cultivation/xianxia) — [腾讯新闻/21经济网 2026-07](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20260715/herald/2918781b1a6278de1575a91dee227582.html)

### Inferences
- Evidence on genre fit is consistent: AI wins where spectacle beats nuance and where the audience is young and male (the 漫剧/web-novel crowd). Live-action holds female-oriented romance and family drama, plus the premium paid tier. Regulators favoring live-action may reinforce this split.
- Expect 2026 H2 to bring a shakeout: lower coefficients, mandatory filing and a 0.12–1% hit rate. Volume growth should slow while top AI studios consolidate.
