# 中国国内微短剧市场（2024–2026）

> Method note (for the report writer): Research date 2026-10-05. Direct page fetches were blocked by the network proxy for almost every Chinese and international source (thepaper, 199it, sina, jiemian, caixin, news.cn, dfcfw PDFs, tvoao, trade.gov). **Every finding below comes from search-result snippets/summaries, not from reading the full articles.** Figures are attributed to the outlet or report named in the snippet. Treat precise numbers as "as reported" and check them before using them in headlines. RMB figures are 亿元 (100 million yuan). Some search summaries wrongly turned "亿" into "billion" (e.g. "3.04 billion MAU"). I converted these back: 3.04亿 = 304 million.

## Q1. Dominant themes and how they shifted, 2023→2026

### Takeaway
Early (2023–2024) micro-dramas ran on fixed "爽" templates: 霸总 for women's titles (女频), 战神/赘婿/神医 comebacks for men's titles (男频), plus 甜宠 and 复仇. After NRTA's late-2024 管理提示 on 霸总 and 中老年 content, and as the free/ad-supported audience grew older and broader, 2025 moved toward 逆袭 + 打脸虐渣 hybrids, female-growth/"no-romance" women's titles, 年代/家庭 warmth, 银发 (older-protagonist) stories, and realism. 2026 adds a big wave of AI-generated 漫剧/AI剧.

### Cited Findings
- DataEye 2025 annual report: "逆袭" was the No.1 theme by both heat (热度值 143.9亿) and title count (5,178 dramas). "现代" and "打脸虐渣" each exceeded 125亿 heat and 5,200+ titles — [DataEye《2025年微短剧行业数据报告》(Jan 2026, via dfcfw PDF)](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202601211818183880_1.pdf); [fxbaogao listing](https://www.fxbaogao.com/detail/5229969)
- The 银发 (silver-hair) theme rose: on the DataEye 短剧热力榜, the No.1 drama 《白首归乡路》 (silver theme) added nearly 3,000万 heat in one day; 麦芽 and 河马 were strong on the chart — [DataEye热力榜 via 澎湃 (2025)](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_30501550). Silver-themed titles such as 《男人五十》 and 《怦然心动五十岁》 were also translated for overseas markets — [DataEye海外热榜 via 白鲸出海](https://www.baijing.cn/article/54363)
- Commentary on 2025 says "霸总不再霸屏，上头转向走心": the classic 霸总 redemption plot is fading, and stories now bring in female independence and real-life issues. 男频 staples such as "霸总+契约婚姻", "战神归来" and "神医赘婿" have clearly lost heat — [腾讯新闻/评点文章, 2025-07-04](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250704A0520O00); [tvoao《微短剧，"霸总"退潮了？》](https://www.tvoao.com/a/220339.aspx)
- 女频 examples of the shift: 《好一个乖乖女》 (female self-salvation/revenge), 《盛夏芬德拉》 (mutual rescue rather than slapping down a 霸总), 《真千金她是学霸》 (no romance line, knowledge changes fate). 男频 titles are adding power struggles, ensemble casts and realistic settings instead of pure "cheat-code" comebacks — [same 2025 评点](https://m.sohu.com/a/910633281_121627717); [lmtw《微短剧2025:流量退潮后…》](https://lmtw.com/mzw/content/detail/id/250525)
- 2025 hit types: 女频爽文 (穿越+逆袭+女强, e.g. the 《十八岁太奶奶驾到》 series), 总裁/甜宠 with 萌宝 and 先婚后爱, 现实向, 家国情怀, 乡村重生, and warm family 年代 stories (e.g. 《家里家外2》, a blended family over 1986–1998). The top title, 《十八岁太奶奶驾到，重整家族荣耀1》, had 44亿+ valid plays; every TOP5 title passed 35亿; urban (都市) settings were about 50% of titles — [澎湃《2025 微短剧行业分析：爆款逻辑、题材迭代与未来趋势》](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_32280720)
- "萌宝" appeared 94 times in an analysis of 6,000 drama titles. 萌宝流 is a hot 女频 subgenre — [same 澎湃 analysis](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_32280720); a title-mining study of 3万 drama names found recurring patterns: upper-class personas, comeback plots, "emotion business" — [腾讯新闻, 2025-05-12](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250512A08Z3900)
- Niche genres that broke out in early 2025: 末世, 药神 and absurdist ("抽象") themes — [腾讯新闻, 2025-01-10](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250110A03XJO00)
- Regulation reshaped themes. NRTA's 管理提示 (2024-11-22) told 霸总 dramas to follow realism and avoid making "爽点" out of money worship and showing off wealth. The 中老年 管理提示 told creators to drop the "恶婆婆/恶奶奶" stereotypes, encouraged filial piety, neighbourly help and "银发力量", and led to takedowns of titles such as 《27岁霸总爱上45岁保洁阿姨》 and 《五十岁的我怀了二十岁霸总的孩子》 — [澎湃](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_29453520); [tvoao 219654](https://www.tvoao.com/a/219654.aspx); [21经济网 2024-11-26](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20241126/herald/cca25234628e16e3b4572a8f9940f4a1.html)
- 2026: AI 漫剧/AI剧 became the main growth engine. About 22.19万 new native AI剧/漫剧 launched on Douyin in H1 2026, but only 1.3% reached the 5,000万-play break-even line (roughly 1 in 77) — [慧动创想 2026H1 AI剧漫剧报告 via 知乎](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/2070176014383296993); [腾讯新闻《泡沫已破！AI漫剧半年爆量22万部 98.7%血本无归》2026-09-07](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260907A0CJS900)
- An English-language critique asks whether the "精品化" (premium) shift is real or just surface polish — [The World of Chinese, Dec 2025](https://www.theworldofchinese.com/2025/12/chinas-micro-dramas-really-evolving/)

### Inferences
- The 2023 → 2026 path looks like this: 2023–24 霸总/战神/赘婿 templates (driven by paid traffic buying); late 2024 regulatory warnings; 2025 逆袭 + 打脸 hybrids, 女性成长, 年代/家庭, 银发, 萌宝, realism; 2026 AI 漫剧 flood plus higher-budget "精品" live-action.
- The rise of 银发 themes fits the audience data (Q2): WeChat mini-programs reach users aged 51+ (31.2% of their users), and short-drama apps have 4,653万 silver users.
- 男频 vs 女频: 女频 seems to have produced more of the 2025 breakout hits (太奶奶, 乖乖女, 盛夏芬德拉). 男频 is moving away from 战神/赘婿 toward ensemble, power-struggle and 都市 stories. I found no precise 男频/女频 share figure.

### Gaps
- No exact 男频 vs 女频 share of titles or revenue for 2025/2026, and no year-by-year theme-share time series (I could not open the DataEye PDF).
- No hard data on how big 战神/赘婿 still are in 2026; their decline is backed by commentary, not numbers.

## Q2. Market size, user count, comparison with box office

### Takeaway
Market size went from 36.8亿 (2021) to 101.7亿 (2022), 373.9亿 (2023) and 505亿 (2024), when it first passed the 2024 film box office of 425.02亿. 2025 estimates depend heavily on scope: about 634–678亿 (艾媒, live-action domestic) versus "over 1,000亿" (中国网络视听协会, and DataEye for micro-drama + 漫剧 output value). Users are about 6.6–7.2亿, roughly 60–70% of netizens.

### Cited Findings
- 2021/2022/2023 market size: 36.8亿 / 101.7亿 / 373.9亿. 2024: 505亿, the first year above the domestic box office (425.02亿). Forecasts: 634.3亿 for 2025 and 856.5亿 for 2027. 2024 users: 5.76亿 (52.4% of netizens), more than food delivery, online literature or ride-hailing — [《2024中国微短剧产业研究报告》(中国网络表演(直播与短视频)行业协会/中国网络视听协会 et al.), reported 2025-03 via 腾讯新闻](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250305A06F6700); [光明网 2025-08-17](https://m.gmw.cn/2025-08/17/content_1304112984.htm); [虎嗅](https://www.huxiu.com/moment/1113456.html)
- 2025 actual (艾媒): 677.9亿. A DataEye-linked summary says this is lower than other estimates because it leaves out overseas revenue and brand-custom dramas — [艾媒咨询](https://www.iimedia.cn/c400/107059.html); [DataEye 2025 report summary](https://www.fxbaogao.com/detail/5229969)
- 中国网络视听协会《中国网络视听发展研究报告（2026）》 (released ~2026-04-15/16): 2025 micro-drama market size passed 1,000亿 (a "千亿赛道"), with 1,200亿 forecast for 2026. Micro-drama users: 6.62亿 (59.7% of netizens). Average daily use: 129 minutes per user, more than long video, making micro-drama the No.2 audiovisual sub-category after short video. Total online audiovisual users: 10.99亿 — [新华网 2026-04-16](https://www.news.cn/politics/20260416/cac5d121fdbf4dd88a92f22bc2218e9d/c.html); [界面新闻](https://www.jiemian.com/article/14255929.html); [新浪 2026-04-15](https://finance.sina.cn/stock/jdts/2026-04-15/detail-inhupzpc8492317.d.html); [199IT](https://www.199it.com/archives/1822475.html)
- DataEye 2025 annual report: total 2025 output of micro-drama + 漫剧 reached about 1,000亿, far above the earlier ~600亿 expectation and close to twice the film box office. The free (IAA) live-action segment was about 533亿 (+113% YoY), about two-thirds of live-action micro-drama. New releases hit a record of nearly 4万 titles — [DataEye 2025 report](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202601211818183880_1.pdf); [澎湃《2025微短剧最新数据：免费规模350亿，新剧近4万创新高》](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_31132177) (that headline's "350亿" free figure appears to be an earlier, partial-year number)
- User counts:
  - 6.96亿 as of June 2025 (nearly 70% of netizens; more than 60% under 40) — [中国网络视听协会 white paper data, via 澎湃/DataEye summaries](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_31132177). This **conflicts with** the 6.62亿 in the 2026 CNSA report above, probably because the two use different definitions.
  - QuestMobile: industry MAU of 7.18亿 in Feb 2026 (apps 3.35亿, +74.3% YoY; WeChat mini-programs 4.27亿, +59.9% YoY) — [QuestMobile 2026短剧行业洞察报告, 2026-04-08](https://www.questmobile.com.cn/research/report/2041710682848727041/)
  - QuestMobile marketing report (Jul 2026) says "8亿用户" — [腾讯新闻 2026-07-28](https://view.inews.qq.com/a/20260728A03BU900); a Sina piece (2026-07-28) headlines "中国8.5亿人看短剧" — [新浪财经](https://finance.sina.com.cn/stock/t/2026-07-28/doc-inikksxz1847241.shtml)
- MPA/Deadline (Sept 2025): China micro-drama revenue would pass the domestic box office in 2025, reaching US$9.4B with 830M+ viewers (up from US$0.5B in 2021 and US$7B in 2024), and is projected to exceed US$16.2B by 2030 — [Deadline, Sept 2025](https://deadline.com/2025/09/micro-drama-revenue-china-exceed-box-office-mpa-1236545453/)
- An academic/policy view calls it "高度关注微短剧产业爆发增长现象" (Feb 2026) and cites the 2025 market as passing 670亿 and overtaking box office — [china-cer.com.cn 2026-02-09](http://www.china-cer.com.cn/lundao/20260209537.html)

### Inferences
- The 2025 figure depends on scope. ~634–678亿 covers domestic live-action (艾媒). ~1,000亿+ appears to include 漫剧/AI剧, brand-custom content and possibly overseas revenue (CNSA, DataEye). The report should state which scope it uses.
- Growth is slowing: +~250% in 2023, ~+35% in 2024, then +34% (艾媒 basis) or ~+98% (CNSA basis) in 2025. 2026 is forecast at 1,200亿 (+20% on the CNSA basis).
- Micro-drama passed the film box office in 2024 and kept that lead in 2025 on every estimate.

### Gaps
- I found no CNNIC-specific micro-drama user figure for end-2025 (could not reach the CNNIC report).
- No authoritative 2025 split of paid (IAP) versus free (IAA) revenue beyond DataEye's "免费 ~533亿 ≈ 2/3 of live-action".

## Q3. Platforms and performance; IAP → IAA shift

### Takeaway
ByteDance's free, ad-supported 红果短剧 dominates. It reached 3.04亿 MAU in Feb 2026, and its DAU went past 1亿 in Jan 2026 and 1.68亿 by about Aug–Sep 2026, more than 爱奇艺 + 腾讯视频 + 优酷 + 芒果TV combined. The industry has moved from paid mini-programs (IAP, financed by buying traffic) to free IAA and hybrid models. 快手 (incl. 喜番) and WeChat mini-programs are the other main channels.

### Cited Findings
- 红果短剧 (ByteDance, launched Aug 2023):
  - Feb 2026 MAU 3.04亿 (about 30x in 2.5 years), 125 minutes average daily use per user. No.2 app 河马剧场 had only 3,928万 MAU — [QuestMobile via 199IT](https://www.199it.com/archives/1820784.html); [腾讯云开发者社区 (红果短剧月活3.04亿, 红果漫剧2400万)](https://cloud.tencent.com/developer/news/3798932)
  - DAU passed 1亿 around Jan/Feb 2026 — [腾讯新闻 2026-02-13](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260213A05KSV00)
  - DAU reached 1.68亿 (reported 2026-09-09), +107% YoY, more than the combined DAU of 爱优腾芒. All four long-video platforms saw double-digit DAU declines, with monthly usage time down 24%–47%. The model is "免费观看 + 广告分账" — [界面新闻](https://www.jiemian.com/article/15072049.html); [腾讯新闻 2026-09-09](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260909A07NXZ00); [新浪财经 2026-09-09](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-09-09/doc-inirfums3626146.shtml); [钛媒体](https://www.tmtpost.com/8136978.html); [21经济网 2026-09-23](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260923/herald/ab73e123ba363bdc99e2d5d8abee22b2.html)
  - Earlier: about 2亿 MAU (36氪, "正在考虑怎么赚更多的钱") — [36氪](https://36kr.com/p/3389966015725704); 2.36亿 MAU in Sept 2025, above 优酷 and B站 (per a Caixin Global cover story summary) — [Caixin Global 2025-11-10](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2025-11-10/cover-story-chinas-micro-dramas-steal-the-show-and-momentum-seems-unstoppable-102380821.html)
  - Regulators suspended 红果 for 5 days in late 2024, and it removed 130 dramas — [Caixin Global/Deadline summary](https://deadline.com/2025/09/micro-drama-revenue-china-exceed-box-office-mpa-1236545453/)
  - 红果免费漫剧 had 2,400万 MAU, with 50% of new users coming from 红果短剧 — [澎湃](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_32928828)
  - July 2026: two ByteDance apps (红果短剧 and presumably 红果漫剧/related) ranked top 2 — [新浪财经 2026-07-28](https://finance.sina.com.cn/stock/t/2026-07-28/doc-inikksxz1847241.shtml)
- QuestMobile (Feb 2026), apps vs mini-programs: 31–50 year-olds are 54.9% of app users, who average 22.1 hours per month. 51+ users are 31.2% of WeChat mini-program users. Short-drama apps have 4,653万 silver users. Top mini-programs by MAU: 掌阅追剧 (~7,411万), 九夕剧场 (5,083万), 茶树短剧 (4,803万), 新美九天 (4,000万), 火红剧场 (3,762万), 卷卷剧场 (3,742万) (names translated back from an English summary; verify exact Chinese names) — [QuestMobile/adquan](https://www.adquan.com/article/359716); [36氪](https://36kr.com/p/3757535229313536)
- 快手: the 《2025快手短剧白皮书》 (Aug 2025) says 快手短剧 DAU passed 3亿 (note: likely means DAU of users who watch short dramas inside the Kuaishou app). 2024 paying users grew 4x YoY. Kuaishou stopped commercial ad placements for third-party mini-programs in early 2024 and moved to native paid content. It now offers IAP, IAA, revenue sharing (分账) and brand-custom dramas — [经济参考报/新华社 2025-08-08](http://jjckb.xinhuanet.com/20250808/5a312d126cbb41dfa0535e08ce845894/c.html)
- Revenue-sharing competition: 抖音 offered a "15亿保底、80%分成" premium track. 快手 offered 8亿 revenue-share funds + 2亿 cash incubation + 10亿 dedicated traffic (快手 "20亿押注分账"). Both launched "短剧会员" (membership) models in 2025 — [tvoao 224226](https://www.tvoao.com/a/224226.aspx); [运营派](https://www.yunyingpai.com/news/1053215.html)
- 抖音 native IAA grew fast and was a key driver of the free market — [DataEye 2025 summary](https://www.fxbaogao.com/detail/5229969)
- Industry structure: three anchors, ByteDance (红果), Tencent (微信视频号/小程序) and Kuaishou (喜番). Premium micro-dramas now have budgets of about US$400k–600k — [Deadline/MPA, 2025](https://deadline.com/2025/09/micro-drama-revenue-china-exceed-box-office-mpa-1236545453/)
- Long-video platforms (爱奇艺, 腾讯视频, 优酷): 325 horizontal (横屏) micro-dramas went live on major general video platforms in Jan–Aug 2025 (+24.52% YoY) — [中国网络视听协会《中国微短剧行业发展白皮书（2025）》 via 澎湃](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_31132177)
- 2026 H1 paid market: weak in Jan–Apr, with February revenue at the half-year low. Reasons given: less overseas ad spending around Spring Festival, cuts to live-action production, and some small platforms exiting. Recovery started from May, led by AI剧/漫剧 — [2026上半年 reports via 知乎/东方财富](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/2070176014383296993)

### Inferences
- The shift from IAP to IAA is mostly complete for the mass market: free content is about 2/3 of live-action revenue (2025) and 红果 leads the market. Paid mini-programs (点众, 九州, 麦芽, 掌阅-type players) are under pressure and moving to hybrid models (混变) and overseas, and 河马 is far behind 红果 (3,928万 vs 3.04亿 MAU).
- The long-video platforms' role has shrunk to premium horizontal micro-dramas and membership bundles. 红果's DAU passing 爱优腾芒 combined is the key 2026 signal.

### Gaps
- No verified 2025/2026 revenue figures for 点众, 九州, 麦芽 or 中文在线 (search snippets did not include them).
- No disclosed 红果 revenue or ad income, and no 微信视频号-specific micro-drama numbers.
- Some mini-program names came from an English translation of a QuestMobile summary, and the original Chinese names may differ.

## Q4. Regulation (国家广电总局 NRTA) and its effects

### Takeaway
Regulation has tightened in steps: a 2022–23 cleanup, then the Feb 2024 tiered review and filing regime (所有微短剧须审核备案 from 2024-06-01), then theme-specific 管理提示 (霸总, 中老年 in Nov 2024; 儿童 in Jan 2026), a Feb 2025 notice requiring licence/filing numbers to be displayed, new tier thresholds in Jan 2026, mandatory filing for AI 动画 micro-dramas by 2026-03-31, and a draft 《微短剧发展管理办法》 (put out for comment on 2026-06-24) with filing publication and distribution licensing. The effect has been to push out low-quality and vulgar content and to speed up the move to "精品化" and realistic themes.

### Cited Findings
- 2022 end – 2023 early: a special cleanup removed 2.53万 micro-dramas (140万 episodes). In 2024 NRTA removed 72 non-compliant mini-programs and dealt with 8,737 vulgar or harmful micro-dramas — [网易/合规指南](https://www.163.com/dy/article/L4NAGB5L05561XT2.html); [澎湃](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_29453520)
- 2024 tiered system (分类分层审核): 重点 ≥100万 yuan investment, 普通 30–100万, 其他 <30万. From 2024-06-01, micro-dramas that have not been reviewed and filed may not be distributed online — [网易/合规指南 summary](https://www.163.com/dy/article/L4NAGB5L05561XT2.html)
- 2025-02-05: NRTA 办公厅 notice 《关于进一步统筹发展和安全促进网络微短剧行业健康繁荣发展的通知》, requiring micro-dramas to show their licence number or filing number, and enforcing 先审后播 (review before broadcast) and platform responsibility — [NRTA官网](https://www.nrta.gov.cn/art/2025/2/5/art_113_70148.html); [央视网 2025-02-05](https://news.cctv.com/2025/02/05/ARTIqNE4cmoNb3hA4mK4CAQI250205.shtml)
- 2024-11-22 管理提示 on 霸总 (realism; no money worship or wealth flaunting as 爽点), and a 中老年 管理提示 (against 恶婆婆 stereotypes) with takedowns of absurd older-protagonist dramas. A separate rule banned certain kinds of titles — [21经济网](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20241126/herald/cca25234628e16e3b4572a8f9940f4a1.html); [tvoao 219795](https://www.tvoao.com/a/219795.aspx); [tvoao 219999](https://www.tvoao.com/a/219999.aspx)
- NRTA ran another 1-month special rectification of online micro-dramas — [tvoao 216541](https://www.tvoao.com/a/216541.aspx)
- 2025-09-25: NRTA announced it was drafting 《微短剧管理办法》. In Feb 2026, at a State Council briefing, it said it would issue 《微短剧发展管理办法》. On 2026-06-24 a draft went out for public comment: three-category management, filing publication plus a distribution licence system, and a ban on promoting money worship — [财新 2026-06-24](https://companies.caixin.com/2026-06-24/102457046.html)
- Jan 2026 tier thresholds raised: 重点 = ≥300万 investment or special themes (politics, military, diplomacy, national security); 普通 = 100–300万; 其他 = <100万 — [宝鸡市文旅局 2026-01-15](https://whhlyj.baoji.gov.cn/col3468/col3494/202601/t20260115_1240723.html); [tvoao 分类标准调整](https://www.tvoao.com/a/223120.aspx)

## Q5. Growth outlook for 2026

### Takeaway
Consensus for 2026 is continued but slower growth: about 1,200亿 (CNSA forecast, +20%). Growth is driven by IAA/free platforms (红果), AI 漫剧/AI剧 (≈400亿 forecast) and brand marketing. Headwinds are weak paid revenue, AI-content oversupply with poor returns, and tighter licensing.
