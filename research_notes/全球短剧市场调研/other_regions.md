# Short Drama / Micro-Drama Markets Outside China & North America (2024–H1 2026)

> Research method note (2026-10-05): Most primary pages (sensortower.com, 36kr.com, thepaper.cn, baijing.cn, cnbc.com, hollywoodreporter.com, koreaherald.com, antom.com, warc.com, nationthailand.com, broadbandtvnews.com) were blocked by the network egress proxy, so figures below come from search-engine result snippets/summaries of those pages, not full-text reads. Every figure is attributed to the page the snippet came from; treat exact numbers as "as reported" and verify against originals before final publication.

## Global overseas totals, regional share and growth

### Takeaway
Overseas (ex-China) short-drama IAP is maturing in revenue (roughly +13–20% YoY by H1/Q1 2026) while downloads are still exploding (+79% to +140% YoY), with download growth almost entirely coming from SEA, LatAm and India. Revenue remains concentrated in the US, Japan, UK/Canada and other developed markets; downloads are concentrated in India, Indonesia and Brazil.

### Cited Findings
**Sensor Tower (global ex-China app data)**
- Q1 2024 global short-drama IAP revenue was US$178M; Q1 2025 was nearly US$700M (~4x). Q1 2025 downloads exceeded 370M, 6.2x Q1 2024. — [Webull/Sensor Tower, 2025](https://www.webull.ca/news-detail/12816921610159104)
- US Q1 2025 revenue was nearly US$350M, 49% of global short-drama app revenue that quarter. — [Webull/Sensor Tower, 2025](https://www.webull.ca/news-detail/12816921610159104)
- Q1 2026 IAP revenue reached about US$750M (+20% YoY), and downloads passed 850M (+140% YoY). — [Sensor Tower, State of Short Drama Apps 2026](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-short-drama-apps-2026-report)
- Q1 2026 download growth was concentrated in SEA, LatAm and India, which together made up more than three-quarters of global downloads: SEA 32%, LatAm 23%, India 22%. All three grew by triple digits YoY. — [Sensor Tower via search summary, 2026](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-short-drama-apps-2026-report); also cited by [Vitrina](https://vitrina.ai/blog/micro-drama-distribution-platforms-2026/)
- In 2025, downloads of the top 20 short-drama apps in Latin America rose about 402% YoY. In Q4 2025, short dramas took nearly 16% of total video-entertainment time in LatAm, the fastest-growing region. — [Sensor Tower State of Short Drama Apps 2026](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-short-drama-apps-2026-report)
- Sensor Tower data shows micro-dramas grew 478% globally, driven by Southeast Asia (headline only; full text not accessible). — [WARC](https://www.warc.com/en/feed/micro-dramas-478-global-growth-driven-by-southeast-asia-sensor-tower-data-shows-c2c35640a9574f778e9d507925c71479)

**Omdia**
- 2025 micro-drama IAP revenue was US$2.98B. Omdia forecasts total micro-drama revenue of US$14B by end-2026, with US$3B of it outside China. — [Omdia via Broadband TV News, 2026-05-29](https://www.broadbandtvnews.com/2026/05/29/uk-leads-european-microdrama-market-says-omdia/)
- Micro-drama share of video time is just 5% in North America and 4% in Europe, which Omdia calls the "largest underpenetrated revenue opportunity". US, Japan and UK were the top 3 by revenue, together contributing over 50%. — [DataEye via 澎湃/36氪, Jan 2026](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_32432287); [36氪](https://36kr.com/p/3648633863893505)
- 2025 Brazil ranked #6 by revenue and #3 by downloads. — [DataEye 2025 observation](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_32432287)
- H1 2026: IAP was about US$1.27B (+13% YoY) and downloads about 1.441B (+79% YoY, but only +20% vs H2 2025). — [DataEye via 界面新闻, Jul 2026](https://www.jiemian.com/article/14845347.html); [百晶](https://www.baijing.cn/article/56268)
- H1 2026 revenue by country: US, Japan and Canada were the top 3, together >44%. Developed markets (Europe/US, Japan/Korea, Australia, Canada) made up >60% of revenue. — [DataEye/界面](https://www.jiemian.com/article/14845347.html)
- H1 2026 downloads by country: India, Indonesia and Brazil were the top 3, together >50%. LatAm, SEA and India made up >70% of downloads. The top 20 apps took 84% of downloads. — [DataEye/澎湃](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_33708735)
- H1 2026: Chinese-owned apps earned about US$1.182B (93%), and local (non-Chinese) overseas apps only about US$70M (7%). — [DataEye via search summary](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4880360.html)
- Full-year 2026 forecast: >US$6B overseas micro-drama market, with app IAP plus free (ad) models >US$3B. — [DataEye/界面](https://www.jiemian.com/article/14845347.html)

- Q1 2025 (DataEye): more than half of titles were translated dramas, and income exceeded 2.4B (unit as reported). Downloads were 259M. — [腾讯新闻, 2025-04-14](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250414A06V3B00)

### Inferences
- The overseas market is splitting in two. Revenue comes from US, Japan, UK, Canada, Australia and Korea. Volume (and ad-funded free models such as Melolo) comes from India, Indonesia, Brazil, Mexico and the Philippines.
- Revenue growth slowed sharply from about 4x (2024→2025) to about 13–20% (2025→2026), while downloads keep growing. That points to falling ARPU as the mix moves toward emerging markets and free/ad models.
- Sensor Tower and DataEye differ on scope and method: Sensor Tower Q1 2026 shows US$750M per quarter, while DataEye H1 2026 shows US$1.27B. Don't mix the series.

## Southeast Asia (Indonesia, Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam)

### Takeaway
SEA is the largest download region (32% of Q1 2026 global downloads), and Indonesia is the #1 or #2 download market worldwide. ByteDance's free, ad-supported Melolo dominates Indonesia and the Philippines. Thailand stands out for monetization (top-3 globally by IAP in Q1 2025) and for a local production ecosystem (Kantana, AIS, Viu Shorts).

### Cited Findings
- SEA had 32% of global short-drama downloads in Q1 2026, with triple-digit YoY growth. — [Sensor Tower 2026](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-short-drama-apps-2026-report)
- Indonesia is described as the world's #1 download market for micro-drama apps, but it monetizes at a fraction of North America's rate. — [Vitrina, 2026](https://vitrina.ai/blog/micro-drama-distribution-platforms-2026/); DataEye puts Indonesia #2 for H1 2026 downloads, after India ([界面](https://www.jiemian.com/article/14845347.html))
- Melolo (ByteDance) launched in SEA in Nov 2024. It had over 2.3M downloads in March 2025, more than 70% of them from Indonesia, followed by the Philippines and Vietnam, which together made up more than 90% of its downloads. It offers free short dramas and relies mainly on translated content, with dubbed and subtitled options. — [Insightrackr, Mar 2025](https://blog.insightrackr.com/en/docs/Mar25TopShortDrama)
- Melolo ranks #1 in Indonesia by both downloads and DAU, with a strong ad-supported base. — [Sensor Tower SEA OTT & short drama insight](https://sensortower.com/blog/southeast-asia-ott-short-drama-growth-market-landscape)
- Thailand ranked #6 globally in micro-drama downloads in H1 2025. Thai users spent US$7.7M on IAP in Q1 2025, ranking #3 globally after the US and Japan. — [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/business/economy/40068585) / [Bangkok Post](https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3292294/microdrama-market-takes-asia-by-storm)
- About 9M Thais watch vertical short-drama apps monthly (AIS figure). Kantana Group, a 75-year-old producer, launched a "K-Short Drama" vertical-series project. — [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/business/economy/40068585); [VietnamPlus](https://en.vietnamplus.vn/micro-dramas-offer-new-opportunities-for-thailands-content-creators-post349022.vnp)
- Vietnam's FPT Play launched "FPT Play Mini" in the short-drama space, following ReelShort and FlexTV. Thailand and Vietnam have become rapid-turn production hubs for global platforms, often delivering whole seasons in under 7 days. — [search summary of Antom/Bangkok Post](https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3292294/microdrama-market-takes-asia-by-storm)
- Viu (PCCW) launched "Viu Shorts" with microdramas in Thai and Indonesian, 1–3 minute episodes. — [Variety, 2026](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/asian-streamer-viu-vertical-video-platform-microdramas-1236624972/)
- FlickReels (Singapore-based FARSUN) was #7 short-drama app by downloads in March 2025. — [Insightrackr](https://blog.insightrackr.com/en/docs/Mar25TopShortDrama)
- Indonesian serialized web content themes include family, mysticism and horror, religion, love, and good vs evil (general web-series study, not micro-drama-specific). — [ResearchGate, Indonesian web series themes](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/376848806_MAPPING_INDONESIAN_WEB_SERIES_THEMES_INSIGHTS_INTO_OVER_THE_TOP_CONTENT_DIVERSITY)

### Inferences
- In Indonesia and the Philippines, free/ad-supported models with dubbed Chinese libraries (Melolo) win on scale. Thailand is the SEA market where paid IAP and local production are both viable.
- Religion-sensitive content (Muslim-majority Indonesia) plausibly favors family, revenge and rags-to-riches themes over explicit romance, but I found no micro-drama-specific source confirming content-moderation practice.

### Gaps
- No reliable Philippines-specific or Vietnam-specific revenue figures were found.
- No sourced data on religion-sensitive localization rules in Indonesian micro-dramas.

## Japan

### Takeaway
Japan is the #2 overseas revenue market, after the US, in every 2025–H1 2026 dataset. It is high-ARPU but low-download, and is dominated (~90%) by Chinese platforms (DramaBox, ShortMax, ReelShort). Local apps, led by emole's BUMP, compete with domestic originals and "wait-for-free" manga-app-style monetization.

### Cited Findings
- Japan app revenue was over US$45M in 2024 (more than 20x 2023), about US$70M+ in H1 2025, and over US$160M with more than 20M downloads for full-year 2025. Projected to exceed US$1.2B by 2030. Chinese platforms (DramaBox, ShortMax, ReelShort) hold about 90% share. — [search summary of Antom Japan / 36Kr EU, 2025–26](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3280977856569473); [Antom Japan](https://knowledge.antom.com/streamings-next-frontier-capitalizing-on-the-rise-of-short-dramas-in-the-japan-market)
- One source claims Japan's short-drama market will reach about US$1B in 2026 and be the world's #2. This conflicts with the ~US$160M 2025 app revenue figure above and likely includes broader or ad revenue; treat as unverified. — [scrmbl.com](https://scrmbl.com/post/japanese-micro-dramas)
- DataEye: Japan was #2 by revenue in 2025 (US/Japan/UK top 3) and in H1 2026 (US/Japan/Canada top 3). In May 2025 Japan had about US$11.42M revenue and 2.66M downloads. — [DataEye/36氪](https://36kr.com/p/3648633863893505); [界面](https://www.jiemian.com/article/14845347.html)
- BUMP (emole Inc., launched Dec 2022) had 2.3M+ downloads as of June 2025 and 4M cumulative downloads by July 2026, with 6.5B official SNS views. It claims #1 downloads among domestic Japanese short-drama apps, and was #1 in the App Store Entertainment free chart on 2026-04-03. — [App Store/PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000172.000039336.html); [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000142.000039336.html)

- BUMP's top title for H1 2026 was 『3000万稼げ！』 ("Earn 30 million!"), a money/success theme. — [Beans News](https://news.iromame-beans.jp/article/b721523a-8649-11f1-8c7b-9ca3ba08e13f)

- Chinese studios shoot Japan-local dramas aimed at young women (「お嬢様」 style). — [36Kr EU](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3265412905471365)

### Inferences
- Japan rewards locally shot, Japanese-cast content and manga-app monetization habits. Chinese platforms win through local shoots in Japan rather than dubbing.

India is the #1 overseas download market (H1 2026) and the fastest-scaling local ecosystem, but it monetizes poorly. Domestic players (Kuku TV, Story TV/Eloelo, Quick TV, ShareChat/Moj, STAGE) dominate over Chinese apps, and there is a shakeout under way (Pocket TV shut).

## Dubbed/translated Chinese vs locally shot content; fastest-growing markets

### Takeaway
Translated dramas dominate by count, but locally shot dramas earn more per title. DataEye found translated titles at more than 50% of volume but only about 40% of heat. One claim says 80% of the Q1 2025 top-20 revenue titles were locally produced. Fastest-growing: LatAm, then India and SEA, with triple-digit download growth.

### Cited Findings
